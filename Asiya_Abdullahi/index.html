ABOUT ME


Driven by a fascination with how engineering principles can restore human physiological function, Asiya's current academic and independent research focuses heavily on biomedical engineering—specifically exploring the convergence of artificial intelligence, software robotics, and advanced biomaterials for Total Artificial Heart (TAH) development, 3D bioprinting of vascularized scaffolds and self regulating implants


Her current work is anchored in the Halal-Biodesign framework, a methodology developed to ensure medical implants and devices utilizes high purity, ethically and culturally sensitive materials are utilized to enhance biocompatibility and clinical trust.


Beyond theoretical research, active industry engagement includes founding VeloxDx, health-tech venture addressing critical diagnostic gaps in underserved regions through intelligent, solar-powered medical hardware and software ecosystems.
